## Direction contract

THESIS: A senior HR advisor's intake should feel like a decisive, considered conversation, not a SaaS lead form. This surface refuses the warm-cream/serif/thin-accent "boutique consultancy" default — bordô is not a highlight here, it is a committed, page-scale color that whole question screens are drenched in.

OWN-WORLD: Marfim (#F7F3EE) as the quiet ground; bordô (#5A1E2D) committed at page scale — full-bleed question panels, not thin washes; dourado (#B58C52) as a structural accent (keylines, numerals, selected-state marks), never decorative. Display type is Bricolage Grotesque (confident, slightly idiosyncratic grotesque, replaces Playfair Display entirely). Body/UI type is Hanken Grotesk. Labels, step counters and pricing figures run in Martian Mono — a direct, deliberate callback to the ticket-label device in Patrícia's own SuperHi reference. Options render as large tactile blocks with keyboard-shortcut letters, not thin-bordered cards.

STORY: The lead understands in the first screen that this is still a conversation with a specific senior person (Patrícia), now delivered one decisive question at a time, with enough visual confidence to read as "tech," never as a generic Typeform clone or a generic SaaS onboarding.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed bordô panel, marfim type. Tiny CALI wordmark + Martian Mono step counter top corners. One oversized Bricolage Grotesque question fills the vertical center-left; answers are large tappable blocks anchored bottom-left; a thin dourado progress rule runs the full top edge.

FORM: Committed color strategy (bordô carries 30–60% of the surface at any given screen, alternating full-bordô question screens with marfim ones, never both diluted into one pale wash). No canon/pick — direction pinned directly by the user's explicit correction (2026-11-17): keep bordô bold, change the fonts, one question per screen, no clichéd success screen. seed: none (pinned) — "bordô em destaque... prefiro uma pergunta de cada... seja bem assertivo."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
